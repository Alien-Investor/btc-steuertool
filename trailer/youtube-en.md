## Description

Bitcoin taxes without an upload: BTC Steuertool turns your CSV exports into the tax report for the German tax office, entirely on your device. Cloud tax tools want every transaction on their server. Here there is no upload, no account and no telemetry. The Android app does not even have an internet permission.

BTC Steuertool calculates bitcoin gains for private investors in Germany under § 23 EStG with the FiFo method, the one-year holding period counted by calendar date and the exemption limit (600 euros up to 2023, 1,000 euros from 2024). It reads exports from BitBox, 21bitcoin, Bison, Swissquote, Strike, Pocket and Bisq plus manual buys and sells, and detects every broker from the file contents. Fees paid in bitcoin count as a disposal of the fee amount, gifts and donations as a reduction of holdings. noKYC holdings stay strictly apart: their own FiFo pool, their own internal report, never part of the documents for the tax office. You get a tax report, formal tax evidence and buys and sells as CSV, saved through the save dialog. The reports are in German, they are meant for the German tax office.

After installation the app downloads nothing: calculation core, ECB exchange rates and BTC daily closing prices are built in. The same open-source calculation core runs in the command-line tool and in the free web version. On the Linux desktop the app is a Flatpak without network permission and without file access, with a GPG-signed checksum.

No tax advice: the tool is a calculation aid, responsibility for the tax return stays with you.
